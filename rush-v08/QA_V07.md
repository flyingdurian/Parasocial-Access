## Boundaries and limitations

- The container's local Chromium could not launch due to an operating-system socket restriction. Previous v0.6.1 desktop/phone browser tests are preserved as a historical report, **not** represented as newly executed in v0.7. This release has not completed new viewport-by-viewport responsive checks or physical iPhone Safari testing.
- The stage is a first-pass elevated-angle scene, not a fully free-moving orthographic bird's-eye RTS camera. Crowd interaction depth is limited: ambient visitors move, arrivals queue and dispatched staff travel; venue layout is currently fixed.
- Attendee stories, safety incidents and arguments are fictional and must not be interpreted as real facts about Destiny, DGG or identifiable individuals.
- Deep source-backed DGG lore corpus research and adjudicated source provenance remain a future content milestone; this update only expands original fictional cultural motifs and political-topic variety.
- Live Arena overflow report was inspected once but not reproduced or fixed; v0.6.1 remains frozen.
- Release is preview-only; previous production URL is untouched.
